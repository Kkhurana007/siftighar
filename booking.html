Escape the hustle and bustle of city life and immerse yourself in the serene beauty of Punjab’s countryside. At Sifti Ghar, we offer a unique blend of agro-ecotourism, cultural experiences, and hands-on farm activities that promise unforgettable memories.

Ready to plan your visit? Book your stay with us today!

Why Book with Sifti Ghar?

  • Authentic Rural Experience: Live the farm life, from milking cows to harvesting crops.
  • Cultural Immersion: Celebrate local festivals, enjoy traditional music, and savor organic Punjabi cuisine.
  • Adventure and Fun: Enjoy camel rides, bull cart rides, and rural games like kabaddi and kite flying.
  • Sustainability: Support eco-friendly tourism and sustainable farming practices.
  • Perfect for All Ages: Whether you’re a family, couple, or group of friends, we have something for everyone.
